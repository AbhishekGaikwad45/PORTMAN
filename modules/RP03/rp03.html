How these ETAs are worked out

No ETA is stored anywhere in the system, so each trip is placed at its latest stamped milestone plus the median time this port has actually taken from that milestone to arrival. Hover any chip for its p10–p90 window. Medians are recomputed from completed trips on every page load; a stage needs at least 30 past voyages before it is used.
